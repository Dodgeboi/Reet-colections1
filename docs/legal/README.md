# Legal templates

These are **starting templates**, not legal advice. They cover the documents a typical
online store publishes. Before you launch — especially since you'll be taking payments and
collecting customer data — **have them reviewed by a qualified attorney** and adapt them to
your business, your state (Illinois), and the countries you ship to.

Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Then publish each as a page on the site (e.g. `/privacy`,
`/terms`, `/shipping-returns`) and link them in the footer.

Files:
- `PRIVACY-POLICY.md`
- `TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md`
- `SHIPPING-AND-RETURNS.md`
