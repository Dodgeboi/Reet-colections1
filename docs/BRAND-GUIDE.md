# Brand guide

## Essence

**Reet Collections** — a warm, family-run boutique for Indian ethnic & fusion wear,
revealed live every evening. Premium but personal; rooted in tradition.

**Tagline:** *Selected with love & care, just for you.*
**Devanagari accent:** स्नेह से चुना गया ("chosen with love").

## Logo

- The gold circular **R/C monogram** is the primary mark.
- In the header it sits in the **top-left corner** beside the "Reet Collections" wordmark.
- On dark backgrounds use the light variant; on light backgrounds, the standard mark.
- Give it clear space; don't stretch or recolor it.

## Typography

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond (headlines, product names) — elegant serif.
- **Body / UI:** Jost (clean sans).
- **Devanagari:** Noto Serif Devanagari (cultural accents like उत्सव, परंपरा, अवसर).

## Motifs & cultural elements

Restraint is the rule: photography and type carry the design; ornament is rare
and deliberate.

- **Gold hairline** (`GoldThread`) — a single fine rule under headings.
- **Diamond divider** (`MotifDivider`) — two hairlines meeting at a small gold diamond.
- **Devanagari accents** appear once per section as kickers (not repeated everywhere).
- **Occasion theming** — flat jewel-tone panels: maroon (bridal), gold (festive),
  rose (everyday), plum (party/sangeet).
- No gradients-as-decoration, no background patterns, no rounded "cards" —
  square corners and hairline borders throughout the storefront.

## Buttons

Flat, sharp-cornered, uppercase with wide letter-spacing:

- `.btn-gold` — primary: solid onyx, ivory text (hovers to gold).
- `.btn-rose` — live / secondary CTAs: solid deep rose.
- `.btn-outline` — quiet, hairline-bordered.
- `.btn-light` / `.btn-light-outline` — for use on photography and dark bands.

## Voice principles

- Warm and human; celebrate the customer and the occasion.
- Cultural, not costume — authentic touches woven in tastefully.
- Say each thing **once**, in the right place. No repetition, no filler, no "AI" sheen.
